# POOL-SCREEN: the offence screen of 2026-09-27

Subsystem DEFINE wrote this file as part of the amendment to `spec/CONTRACT.md` Q4, and it is part of the contract. It lists every species the screen put OUT, every species it added, and the numbers of the rewritten `spec/pool.csv`.

Josh ruled that no taker may get a Pokémon that could read as offensive or upsetting, and that the lean filter goes. The rule is in Q4. A species is OUT if its name, its genus, its Pokédex text, its design or its common community read makes a plausible joke about the taker's looks, weight, age, intelligence, hygiene, laziness, attractiveness, temper or worth. The genus counts because the result screen shows it. A species that stays can still carry line bans, and Q4 lists them for every pool member.

The method was the same for every species. A script listed each one's name, genus and every English Pokédex line from `data/corpus.json` that matched a word in the screen categories. I read every hit, then weighed the design and the community read. Calls marked judgement could go either way, and the panelist may overrule them.
