A quick, positive quiz

Which Pokémon are you?

15 short questions. Pick who is taking it.

How it works

The quiz is built on the Big Five, the trait model most personality research uses. The five traits here are Imagination, Planning, Social energy, Getting along and Steadiness. There are 15 questions. Each trait gets three of them. Your answers place you on all five traits, and your Pokémon is the one whose trait profile sits nearest to yours. It is a game drawn from that research and makes no claim to measure you. The sources are in the research note.
